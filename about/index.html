Hi, I'm Guido

I’m a Senior Product Designer with 8+ years of experience creating AI-powered and consumer products used by thousands of people. My work spans conversational AI, marketplaces, fintech, gaming, and social platforms, with a focus on transforming complex technology into experiences that feel simple, trustworthy, and delightful.

I’ve led products from idea to launch across web, iOS, and Android, working closely with cross-functional teams to define strategy, validate ideas through research, and deliver polished, high-quality experiences.

My background combines startup ownership with experience in larger organisations, giving me the ability to move from vision and product strategy to execution and delivery.

Based in London and working remotely with global teams, I enjoy tackling ambiguous problems, collaborating across disciplines, and designing products that create measurable impact for both users and businesses.
